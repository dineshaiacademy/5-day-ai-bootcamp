# Dinesh AI Academy — Student Handbook & Policies

## 1. Program overview
Dinesh AI Academy runs a 5-day, hands-on AI engineering bootcamp covering LLM
fundamentals, retrieval-augmented generation, tool use and workflows, agents and
MCP, and memory/guardrails/evaluation. Sessions are delivered live, with companion
workshop projects for each day.

## 2. Enrollment & prerequisites
- Open to anyone comfortable writing basic Python (variables, functions, loops).
  No prior AI/ML experience required.
- Each participant must bring a laptop capable of running Python 3.10+ and a
  code editor (VS Code recommended).
- Enrollment is confirmed once payment is received and a confirmation email is
  sent.

## 3. Attendance policy
- Each day builds on the previous one — live attendance is strongly recommended.
- A Certificate of Completion requires attending at least 4 of the 5 sessions
  (80% attendance).
- Recordings of each session are provided within 24 hours for anyone who misses
  a live session, so missed content can be caught up before the next day.

## 4. Cancellation & refund policy
- Full refund if cancelled at least 7 days before the bootcamp start date.
- 50% refund if cancelled between 6 and 2 days before the start date.
- No refund for cancellations within 48 hours of the start date, or after the
  bootcamp has begun — a seat transfer to a future cohort may be offered instead,
  at the academy's discretion.
- No refunds are issued for missed sessions once the bootcamp has started;
  recordings remain available as a substitute.

## 5. Code of conduct
- Treat instructors and fellow participants with respect — no harassment,
  discrimination, or disruptive behavior.
- Collaboration during workshop exercises is encouraged; submitting someone
  else's work as your own for a certificate is not permitted.
- Recording or redistributing session content outside the cohort requires prior
  permission from Dinesh AI Academy.
- Violations may result in removal from the program without a refund.

## 6. Certificate of completion — requirements
To receive a Certificate of Completion, a participant must:
1. Attend at least 4 of the 5 live sessions (or watch the recordings within 48
   hours of each missed session).
2. Submit the Day 5 capstone workshop project.
3. Follow the code of conduct throughout the program.

## 7. Session format & materials
- Each day is a 60-90 minute live session followed by a self-paced workshop
  project with starter code and a session-plan guide.
- All workshop materials, slides, and starter repos remain accessible to
  enrolled participants after the bootcamp ends.
- Session plans and projects are organized by day in the shared bootcamp
  repository.

## 8. Hardware & software requirements
- A laptop (Windows, macOS, or Linux) with at least 8GB RAM.
- Python 3.10 or newer installed.
- A free-tier API key for the LLM provider used each day (e.g. Google Gemini,
  OpenAI) — instructions for obtaining one are provided before each session.
- No GPU is required for any workshop in this bootcamp.

## 9. Communication & support
- Questions during the bootcamp are handled in the shared cohort chat channel
  (link provided at enrollment) or by email to the instructor.
- Typical response time for support questions is within 1 business day.
- Post-bootcamp questions about certificates or materials access can be sent to
  the same support email.

## 10. Data & privacy
- Code and projects written during workshops belong to the participant.
- Dinesh AI Academy does not collect or store participants' personal API keys —
  each participant uses their own key, kept locally on their own machine.
- Any participant work shared publicly by Dinesh AI Academy (e.g. as teaching
  examples) will only be used with prior consent.
